Phoenix Point Multiclass & Perk Browser

Pick any two of Phoenix Point's 7 soldier classes and see both classes' full weapon proficiency and perk lists side by side. This page shows what each class pair unlocks. It does not rank class pairs, and nothing here is saved or sent anywhere — it renders from the data in this page in your browser.

Pick a pair

At Level 4, a soldier can acquire a second class. That immediately unlocks the second class's weapon proficiency; its abilities unlock as you spend skill points, same as the primary class.
